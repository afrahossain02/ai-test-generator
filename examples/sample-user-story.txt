Title: Sign in with email and password

As a registered customer
I want to sign in with my email address and password
So that I can access my order history and saved addresses

Acceptance criteria:
- The sign-in form has an Email field, a Password field, and a "Sign in" button.
- On success the customer lands on /account and their name appears in the header.
- On wrong credentials the form shows "Email or password is incorrect" and stays on the page.
- Passwords must be between 8 and 64 characters.
- After 5 consecutive failed attempts for the same email, further attempts are blocked for 15 minutes.
- A "Remember me" checkbox keeps the session alive for 30 days instead of the default 24 hours.
- The password field is never shown in plain text and is never included in any URL.
